I am an experimental physicist and earth scientist, currently a Research Associate Professor in the Department of Meteorology at the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey, California. My research is focused on developing a fundamental understanding of the physical mechanisms coupling the atmosphere and ocean, and how those natural processes impact US Naval operations and activity in the marine environment. I am particularly interested in air-sea exchange and interaction mechanisms, ocean surface wave dynamics, atmospheric turbulence, and boundary layer processes. As an experimentalist, I pursue my research in the field, and in the laboratory, using a variety of platforms and systems to make environmental measurements to analyze earth system processes, evaluate numerical models, and develop new theoretical understanding.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Marine Physics from the University of Miami's Rosenstiel School in 2017 and a B.S. in Physics with a specialization in Earth Science from UC San Diego. My work has been supported by the Office of Naval Research, the National Science Foundation, and other U.S. Navy programs.
