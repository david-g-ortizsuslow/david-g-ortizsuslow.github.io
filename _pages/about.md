I am an experimental physicist and earth scientist, currently a Research Associate Professor in the Department of Meteorology at the Naval Postgraduate School in Monterey, California. My research focuses on developing a fundamental understanding of the physics coupling the atmosphere and ocean, and how this system impacts US Naval operations in the marine environment.

I am particularly interested in air-sea exchange mechanisms, ocean surface wave dynamics, atmospheric turbulence, and boundary layer processes. As an experimentalist, I pursue my research in the field, and in the laboratory, using a variety of platforms and observing systems to study earth system processes, evaluate numerical models, and develop new theoretical understanding.

I received my Ph.D. in Applied Marine Physics from the University of Miami's Rosenstiel School in 2017, and a B.S. in Physics from UC San Diego. My work has been supported by the Office of Naval Research, the National Science Foundation, and other U.S. Navy programs.

See my [publications](/publications/) and [projects](/projects/), or [get in touch](/contact/).
